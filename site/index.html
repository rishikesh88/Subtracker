How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

No imports, no receipts to dig up, no tagging by hand.

01

Connect Gmail

One Google sign-in, read-only. Choose how far back to look — anywhere from seven days to six months.

02

Verloq reads the receipts

It scans message headers to find the receipts, opens only those, and pulls out the service, amount, cadence and renewal date.

03

You approve the list

Nothing is added behind your back. Verloq proposes, flags anything that looks like a duplicate, and you decide what to keep.

What Verloq does with your Gmail

We ask for read access, so we tell you exactly what we read.

Most tools in this category are vague about this. Here is the whole of it, in the same words we use in our privacy policy.

Read-only, always

The Gmail permission Verloq requests cannot send, delete, label or alter anything. Your inbox is left exactly as it was.

Only receipts are opened

Every message gets a header-level look to decide whether it is a receipt. Only the ones that pass have their contents read; the rest are discarded.

Never sold, never advertised against

Your email data is used to build your subscription list and nothing else. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train general AI models.

Leave and it is gone

Disconnect from settings and the Google grant is revoked and your stored data deleted. No retention period, no export request needed.
