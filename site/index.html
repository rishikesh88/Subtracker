How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

No CSV imports, no card feed, no IT project.

01

Connect your billing inbox

One Google sign-in, read-only. Add as many mailboxes as you need — billing@, accounts@, or whichever inbox your vendors actually write to.

02

Verloq reads the receipts

It scans message headers to find vendor receipts, opens only those, and pulls out the vendor, amount, tax, cadence and renewal date.

03

You approve the list

Nothing lands in your ledger unseen. Verloq proposes what it found, and you decide what to keep.

Questions

Clear answers before you connect anything

Which mailbox should we connect?

Whichever one your vendors already write to — usually billing@ or accounts@. You can add more than one, so if receipts are split between a shared inbox and a founder’s inbox, connect both. Verloq reconciles across all of them into a single list.

Do you need our bank or card details?

No. Verloq works entirely from the receipts vendors email you. It has no connection to your bank, your cards, or your accounting system, and it cannot make or see a payment.

Can Verloq change anything in our mailbox?

No. The permission it requests is read-only — it cannot send, delete, label, archive or alter a message. You can revoke it at any time from your Google account settings, independently of us.

What happens to our data if we stop using it?

Disconnecting a mailbox deletes Verloq’s stored tokens and stops it reading that mailbox. The authorisation itself lives in your Google account and you can revoke it there at any time without involving us. To close your account entirely, email us — during the beta we do that by hand and confirm when everything is gone.

Does it work with Outlook or Microsoft 365?

Not yet — Gmail and Google Workspace only for now. Outlook support is written but not enabled. If that is what you need, say so when you join the waitlist and it moves up the list.
